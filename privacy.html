We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress (for example levels, stars, hints and settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or use “Reset progress”.

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;

  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and diagnostic data (crash and performance information).

This data is processed by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy. Data is transmitted encrypted.

3. Your choices and consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google’s consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings → Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data retention and deletion

We do not store any personal data on our own servers. Game progress stays on your device. To request deletion of data held by Google in connection with ads, see Google’s privacy tools at myaccount.google.com, or contact us and we will help.
